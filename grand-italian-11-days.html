Grand Italian Discovery & Coastal Odyssey

11 Days / 10 Nights Trip Code: TD2635

📍 Rome, Sorrento, Capri, Positano, Amalfi, Pompeii, Tuscany, Pisa, Florence, Venice, Padova, Verona, Lake Como, Milan

Embark on the ultimate 11-day Italian odyssey taking you from the imperial monuments of Rome and the sacred treasures of Vatican City to the sun-drenched Amalfi Coast, Capri island, and ancient Pompeii.

🌍 Countries Covered:
Italy Vatican City

Tour Overview & Experience

Experience the magic of Italy on this comprehensive 11-day journey spanning ancient history, sacred landmarks, breathtaking coastal drives, and world-renowned art. Designed with private vehicle transport for maximum comfort, this itinerary brings together Rome, Sorrento, Capri, Positano, Amalfi, Pompeii, Tuscany, Pisa, Florence, Venice, Padova, Verona, Lake Como, and Milan.

Key Tour Highlights

  • Colosseum & Roman Forum entrance
  • Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel ticket
  • Capri Island scenic boat tour
  • Amalfi Coast cliffside towns drive
  • Pompeii Ancient Archaeological Ruins
  • Leaning Tower of Pisa & Florence Duomo
  • Venice Boat Pass & St. Mark's Square
  • Lake Como & Fashion Capital Milan

Trip Specifications

  • Trip Code: TD2635
  • Duration: 11 Days / 10 Nights
  • Accommodation: 10 Nights Hotel + Daily Breakfast
  • Transport: Private Vehicle & Driver Service

✈️ Arrival: Arrive at Rome Airport. Meet your private driver for a comfortable transfer to your hotel.

⛲ Rome Evening: Spend the afternoon discovering the Eternal City’s charming piazzas, including the Trevi Fountain, Spanish Steps, and Piazza Navona.

🏨 Overnight: Rome

🏛️ Ancient Rome: Spend the day exploring imperial monuments, featuring an entry visit to the iconic Colosseum and the Roman Forum.

🏨 Overnight: Rome

🇻🇦 Vatican City: Enter the world-renowned Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel with your included entrance tickets, followed by St. Peter’s Basilica.

🏛️ City Sights: Enjoy an afternoon stroll through the vibrant avenues and historic landmarks of Rome.

🏨 Overnight: Rome

🌊 Capri Island: Transfer south toward the Bay of Naples and take a scenic tour of the fairytale island of Capri to explore its coastline and turquoise waters.

🏨 Overnight: Sorrento

🚘 Coastal Drive: Take a breathtaking coastal excursion along the UNESCO-listed Amalfi Coast, stopping in dramatic cliffside towns including Positano and Amalfi.

🏨 Overnight: Sorrento

🏛️ Pompeii Ruins: Step back in time with entrance tickets to explore the remarkably preserved ancient ruins of Pompeii.

🚗 Transfer: Continue driving north into the rolling hills and vineyards of Tuscany.

🏨 Overnight: Tuscany

📸 Pisa Stopover: Visit the Piazza dei Miracoli to admire and photograph the world-famous Leaning Tower of Pisa.

🎨 Florence Sights: Walk through the historic heart of Florence, viewing the Duomo, Piazza della Signoria, and Ponte Vecchio.

🏨 Overnight: Tuscany

🚤 Venice Sightseeing: Transfer to the lagoon and board a boat with your Venice Boat Pass to explore St. Mark's Square, the Rialto Bridge, and the Bridge of Sighs.

🏨 Overnight: Venice Area

⛪ Padova Visit: Morning stop in Padova to explore its historic heritage and spiritual landmarks.

🎭 Verona Sights: Drive to romantic Verona to walk through its ancient arena and view Juliet’s House.

🏨 Overnight: Verona

🌊 Lake Como & Milan: Discover the glamorous waterfront of Lake Como before exploring Milan's iconic Duomo and Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II.

🏨 Overnight: Milan

✈️ Transfer: Private vehicle transfer to Milan Airport for your departure flight home. End of Services.
