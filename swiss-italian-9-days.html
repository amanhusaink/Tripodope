Tour Overview & Experience

Board the famous Bernina Route Red Train across Alpine passes into Switzerland, experience the dramatic Lauterbrunnen Valley of 72 Waterfalls, scenic Lucerne, Europe's largest Rhine Falls boat cruise, and ascend 3,020 meters on Mt. Titlis Rotair revolving cable car on this exclusive 5-day luxury private tour between Switzerland and Milan.

Key Tour Highlights

  • Bernina Route Red Train (2nd Class) Alpine Railway
  • Mt. Titlis Rotair Revolving Cable Car & Cliff Walk
  • Rhine Falls Boating Experience & Lindt Chocolate Museum
  • Lauterbrunnen "Valley of 72 Waterfalls" & Interlaken
  • Historic Lucerne Chapel Bridge & Lake Como Scenic Drive
  • Milan City Historic Center & Fashion District

Trip Specifications

  • Trip Code: TD2634
  • Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights (13 Sep – 17 Sep)
  • Guests: 03 Adults
  • Transport: Private Vehicle (Day 1 to Day 5)
  • Basis: Bed & Breakfast

✈️ Arrival: Arrive at Milan Airport. Meet your private driver for a comfortable transfer into the city.

🏛️ Milan & Lake Como: Explore Milan's historic center and fashion district before taking a scenic drive to the stunning shores of Lake Como.

🏨 Overnight: — Milan Area

🚂 Bernina Express: Board the famous Bernina Route Red Train (2nd Class) for a spectacular high-altitude railway journey across Alpine passes into Switzerland.

🍫 Zurich: Visit Zurich's historic Old Town before hotel check-in.

🏨 Overnight: — Switzerland

🏞️ Swiss Valleys: Discover the dramatic "Valley of 72 Waterfalls" in Lauterbrunnen and stroll through the scenic alpine resort hub of Interlaken.

🌉 Lucerne Visit: Continue to historic Lucerne to walk across the 14th-century Chapel Bridge and enjoy the lakeside views.

🏨 Overnight: — Switzerland

🌊 Rhine Falls: Visit Europe's largest waterfall for an exciting Rhine Falls boating experience.

🚠 Mt. Titlis: Ascend 3,020 meters aboard the Titlis Rotair revolving cable car to experience the Glacier Cave and Cliff Walk suspension bridge.

🚗 Transfer: Drive south across the Swiss-Italian border to Milan for check-in.

🏨 Overnight: — Milan

✈️ Transfer: Private vehicle transfer to Milan Airport for your return flight home.

🏁 End of Services.
