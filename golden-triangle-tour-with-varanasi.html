Golden Triangle Tour with Varanasi

India

Tour Overview & Experience

The Golden Triangle Is One Of The Most Famous Tourist Routes Of Northern India That Covers Three Different Cities Delhi, Jaipur, And Agra. It Is An Ideal Tour Package For Someone Who Is Traveling To India For The First Time. The Golden Triangle Tour Combined With Varanasi Is One Of The Best Combinations Of Traveling Destinations That Any First-Time Visitor Can Enjoy As It Covers All The Prominent Heritage Sites Of India.

✨ Key Highlights

  • Handpicked experiences and sightseeing
  • Comfortable and premium accommodation
  • Expert guidance and 24/7 support

📋 Essential Summary

  • Trip Code: IN-GTV10
  • Duration: 10 Days / 9 Nights
  • Accommodation: 3/4 Star Hotel or Resort
  • Transfers: Private Car Transfers

You Will Meet Your Company Representative At Delhi International Airport Where They Will Take You To Your Hotel. Here You Will Get To Have An Overnight Stay And Rest Up For The Big Journey Tomorrow.

After Having A Delicious And Healthy Breakfast In The Hotel We Will Begin Our Sightseeing Tour Of Delhi. We Will Begin With Traveling Around The Monuments Of Old Delhi Such As:

Red Fort:

Red Fort Is A Gigantic Fort In The Capital Of India. It Served As The Main Residence Of Royal Family Of The Mughal Empire During Their Reign. It Was Commissioned In 1638 By Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan When They Shifted The Capital Of Empire From Agra To Delhi. Its Unique Architecture Is What Attracts Millions Of Visitors Every Year To Delhi And This Is The First Site That You Are Going To See In Your Tour Package.

Jama Masjid:

Jama Masjid Is One Of The Biggest Mosques Of India That Was Built In 1650- 1656 By Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan. Its Beautiful Architectural Style And Beauty Is What That Will Leave You In Awe During Your Visit To This Majestic Monument.

Chandni Chowk:

Chandni Chowk Is One Of The Most Popular Fast Food Streets Of Northern India. Here You Can Have A Huge Variety Of Mouth-Watering Fast Foods Of India. This Is The Place That People From All Over The Country Visit At Least Once During Their Trip To Delhi As It Has All The Things That One Wishes To Have.

After That, We Will Make A Stop At A Local Restaurant Where You Get To Enjoy Delicious Lunch. After Lunch, We Will Make Our Way To New Delhi Where You Will Get To Explore:

India Gate:

India Gate Is One Of The Most Popular And Important Monuments Of New Delhi. It Is War Memorial Built For More 80,000 Indian Soldiers Who Lost Their Lives During World War 1. It Is Beautiful 42 Meters High Arch That Was Designed By One Of The Most Popular Architecture Of The Time Edwin Lutyens. It Is Also Known As The All India War Memorial.

Humayun’s Tomb:

As The Name Suggests Humayun’s Tomb Is The Tomb Of Mughal Emperor Humayun’s Which Was Commissioned By His First Wife And Consort Bega Begum In 1558. It Has A Unique Blend Of Indo-Persian Architecture That We Are Sure You Are Going To Love.

Qutub Minar:

Qutub Minar Is A UNESCO World Heritage Site Located In The Mehrauli Region Of New Delhi. Standing At The Height Of 72.5 Meters It Is A Minaret And A Victory Tower That Is Made Completely With Bricks Making It One Of The Tallest Brick Tower In The World.

Rashtrapati Bhawan:

At The Western End Of New Delhi You Will Find This Magnificent Building Called Rashtrapati Bhawan. As The Name Suggests This Building Is Used As The Residence Of President Of India.

After This, We Will Return To The Hotel Where You Will Have Dinner And Enjoy An Overnight Stay.

After Breakfast, We Will Leave For Jaipur Early In The Morning. Upon Arrival In The Pink City, Visit Jaigarh And Nahargarh Fort. Overnight Stay At Jaipur Hotel.

We Will Begin Our Jaipur Tour Early And Leave Right After You Are Done Enjoy Your Mouth-Watering Breakfast In Jaipur Hotel. Here In The Pink City, You Will Get To Explore Amer Fort With An Elephant Ride, Hawa Mahal, And Clam And Soothing Ride To Jal Mahal.

Here Is Quick Description Of These Monuments To Give You An Idea What You Will Get To See:

Amer Fort:

Known For Its Artistic Style Elements Amer Fort Is A UNESCO World Heritage Site That Is Located In The Town Of Amer Few Kilometers Away From Jaipur The Capital Of Rajasthan. With Its Cobbled Paths And Huge Series Of Gates, The Fort Gracefully Looks Over The Beautiful Maota Lake, Its Primary Source Of Water. The Architectural Style Of Mughals Greatly Influenced Some Of The Buildings Found Here.

Hawa Mahal:

Made With Red And Pink Sandstone This Beautiful Monument Is Located In The Capital City Of Rajasthan, Jaipur And Extends All The Way To Women’s Chamber Of Zenana. It Is Known For Their Uniquely Designed Window That Allows For Free Circulation Of Air Within The Overall Structure.

Jal Mahal:

Sitting Gracefully Amid The Man Sagar Lake The Jal Mahal Is One Of The Most Beautiful Monuments That You Can See In The Pink City Jaipur. You Will Get To Enjoy A Serene Drive Past Near This Monument During This Golden Triangle Tour Package.

After We Make A Stop For Lunch In A Local Restaurant That We Will Restart Our Sightseeing Tour Where You Will Visit:

Jantar Mantar:

Jantar Mantar Is A Collection Of 19 Different Astronomical Instruments That Were Built By Sawai Jai Singh II. This Monument Was Completed In 1734 And Is Counted Among UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

City Palace:

City Palace Is Another Major Landmark And Tourist Destination Of Jaipur City. It Was Built At The Time Of Formation Of The City Itself When Sawai Jai Singh II Moved His Court From Amber To Jaipur.

After You Are Done Exploring These Monuments You Will Be Taken To The Local Market For Shopping. Then You Will Be Taken Back To The Hotel For An Overnight Stay.

After Breakfast, We Will Check Out From The Hotel And Drive To Agra Via Fatehpur Sikri A UNESCO World Heritage Site. Fatehpur Sikri Is An Old And Abandoned Town That Is Located At The Outskirts Of The Agra City Nearly 45 Kms Away From The City Center. It Has Many Beautiful Examples Of A Long-Forgotten Architectural Style That You Will Get To See During Your Visit There. Upon Your Arrival At Agra, We Will Take You To Your Hotel Where You Can Have An Overnight Stay.

To Get The Perfect Sunset View Of The Taj Mahal We Will Leave The Hotel At 6 AM The Come Back To The Hotel For Breakfast After Exploring Taj Mahal. After Breakfast, We Will Start Our Sightseeing Tour Where You Will Get To Explore:

Taj Mahal:

Taj Mahal is the monument that attracts millions of tourist to India. It was built in 1631-1648 by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in the memories of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Due to its intricate and awe-aspiring design it is counted among one of the Seven Wonders of the World. It is completely made with white marble which during the sunrise orange hue gives it a majestic feel. This is the site that one should never miss-out on during their India trip.

Agra Fort:

Agra Fort Is A Gigantic Monument That Is Located Near The Taj Mahal. It Was Built In Early 15th Century By Mughal Emperor Akbar And Is Considered To Be A UNESCO World Heritage Site. Made With Red Sandstone It Was Initially Constructed As An Army Camp But Later It Was Enlarged And Beautified To Be Used As The Residence Of Royal Family.

Mehtab Bagh:

Mehtab Bagh Is A Beautiful Royal Mughal Garden That Is Located Before The Taj Mahal. This Is The Ideal Spot For You If You Wish To Get The Perfect Shot Of The Sunrise View Of The Taj Mahal.

Itmad-Ud-Daulah:

Also Known As The Baby Taj Or Bacha Taj Itmad-Ud-Daulah Is Another Great Heritage Site That You Will Get To Explore In The City Of Agra. It Captures The Intrinsic Beauty Of Delicate Architectural Style Of The Taj Mahal Pretty Well.

In The Evening We Will Board A Train To Varanasi From Agra Railway Station And You Will Get To Enjoy An Overnight Train Journey.

We Will Arrive At Varanasi By Afternoon; You Will Meet Our Tour Representative At A Railway Station. Your Guide Will Take You To Your Hotel Room Where You Can Rest Up Till Evening. In The Meaning, You Will Get To Enjoy The Aarti Ceremony On The Ghats Followed By An Overnight Stay At The Varanasi Hotel.

After Enjoying A Delicious Breakfast In Your Varanasi Hotel We Will Begin Our Sightseeing Tour. In Varanasi, You Will Get To Explore Various Beautiful Ancient Temples Like:

Kashi Vishwanath Shrine:

This Shrine Is Dedicated To The Hindu Deity Lord Shiva And Is Known For Being One Of The Most Popular Pilgrim Sites Of India. This Beautiful Shrine Is Located At The Bank Of Ganga River That Gives It A Unique And Beautiful Look And Feel.

Bharat Mata Mandir:

Although This Temple Is Called A Shrine It Does Not Have Any Idol Of God Or Goddess In It. This Temple Is Known For A Complete Map Of Undivided India That Is Carved On Its Wall.

Aurangzeb’s Mosque:

Another Beautiful Piece Of Architecture That You Are Going To See During Your Sightseeing Tour To The Holy City Of Varanasi.

All This Followed By A Delightful Boat Ride In The River Ganga. After This, We Will Take You Back To Your Hotel Where You Get An Overnight Stay.

After Breakfast, Your Guide Will Take You For A Sarnath’s Tour Which Is Near Varanasi At A Distance Of 10 Kms From The City Center. Sarnath Is A Beautiful And Holy Buddhist Site Which Is Something That You Should Not Miss Out On During Your Trip To India. In The Afternoon Our Representative Will Take You To The Varanasi Airport Where You Will Board A Flight Back To The Capital Of India Delhi. On Your Arrival In Delhi, Our Representative Will Take You To Your Hotel. Here You Will Get To Rest Up In An Overnight Stay. .

We Will Check Out From The Hotel In The Morning And Your Chauffer Will Take You Back To The Delhi International Airport Where You Can Board Your Flight To Your Next Destination. .
