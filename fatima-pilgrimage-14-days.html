14 Days Pilgrimage

Fatima Feast and European Pilgrimage

"A holy devotional journey through the major shrines and sacred cathedrals of Western Europe."

🗓️ 14 Days 🗼 4 Countries 📍 8 Cities
Back to Packages

About this Itinerary

Embark on a deeply spiritual 14-day Fatima Feast and European Pilgrimage tour. This holy journey takes you across the most significant Marian shrines and sacred sites of Europe. Explore the Sanctuary of Fatima in Portugal, the Massabielle Grotto in Lourdes, France, the holy basilicas of Paris, Vatican City, Rome, and the serene home of St. Francis in Assisi, Italy. A perfect itinerary designed for devotional reflection and cultural exploration.

1

Day 1: Arrival in Lisbon, Portugal

Welcome to Portugal! Land in Lisbon and transfer to your hotel. Spend your day resting or explore Lisbon's colorful historic quarters like Alfama and Baixa.

2

Day 2: Lisbon City Tour & Travel to Fatima

Enjoy a guided sightseeing tour of Lisbon, visiting Jeronimos Monastery, Belem Tower, and Monument to the Discoveries. In the afternoon, travel to the holy site of Fatima.

3

Day 3: Fatima Sanctuary & Devotional Pilgrimage

Spend a full devotional day visiting the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Fatima. Visit the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary, the Chapel of Apparitions, and join the evening candlelit procession.

4

Day 4: Travel to Madrid, Spain

Journey across the Iberian peninsula into Spain, arriving in the majestic capital city of Madrid. Stroll around the beautiful Plaza Mayor and sample Spanish cuisine.

5

Day 5: Madrid Historic Royal Tour

Guided tour of Madrid, exploring the magnificent Royal Palace, Puerta del Sol, and Gran Via. Admire Spain's rich heritage and cultural monuments.

6

Day 6: Travel to Lourdes, France

Cross the Spanish border and travel north through the Pyrenees mountains to the sacred town of Lourdes in southern France, one of the world's most famous Catholic pilgrimage sites.

7

Day 7: Lourdes Grotto & Sanctuary Pilgrimage

Spend a full day in Lourdes. Visit the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes, the sacred Massabielle Grotto where St. Bernadette witnessed the apparitions, take the holy baths, and participate in the candlelit Rosary procession.

8

Day 8: Travel to Paris, France

Board a high-speed train or travel north through the rolling French countryside to the beautiful city of Paris, the City of Light.

9

Day 9: Paris Sightseeing & Sacred Heart Cathedral

Visit the spectacular Basilica of the Sacred Heart (Sacre-Coeur) in Montmartre, view the Notre Dame Cathedral, and enjoy a scenic cruise down the River Seine. View the Eiffel Tower and Arc de Triomphe.

10

Day 10: Travel to Rome, Italy

Fly or travel south into Italy, arriving in Rome, the historic capital and center of the Catholic Church. Check in and enjoy an Italian dinner near the historic center.

11

Day 11: Vatican City & St. Peter's Basilica

Spend the day in Vatican City. Tour the Vatican Museums, stand in awe of the Sistine Chapel's frescoes, and visit St. Peter's Basilica, the largest church in the world.

12

Day 12: Rome Historic Basilicas Devotion

Visit Rome's major patriarchal basilicas: the Basilica of St. Paul Outside the Walls, St. Mary Major, and the Cathedral of St. John Lateran, climb the Holy Stairs (Scala Sancta).

13

Day 13: Travel to Assisi, City of St. Francis

Take a day excursion to the peaceful Umbrian hilltop town of Assisi. Visit the Basilica of St. Francis, view the spectacular medieval frescoes, and enjoy the serene spiritual atmosphere before returning to Rome.

14

Day 14: Departure from Rome, Italy

After a deeply spiritual 14-day European pilgrimage, transfer to Rome Fiumicino Airport for your departure flight back home.
