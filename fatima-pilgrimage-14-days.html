Tour Overview & Experience

Join Tripodope for a captivating journey through Europe's most iconic destinations. Our Jewels of Europe Tour takes you across Italy, France, Switzerland, Spain, and Portugal, blending cultural, spiritual, and historical experiences. From the majestic Vatican City to sacred pilgrimage sites like Lourdes and Fatima, this tour offers an enriching adventure. Visit renowned landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Colosseum, and Mount Titlis, while exploring spiritual gems like the Basilica of St. Francis in Assisi and the Basilica of St. Thérèse in Lisieux. Perfect for history enthusiasts and spiritual seekers alike, this journey promises unforgettable memories and experiences. Let Tripodope guide you through the timeless treasures of Europe!

Key Tour Highlights

  • Vatican City: Papal Audience & St. Peter's Basilica
  • Imperial Rome: Colosseum, Roman Forum & Circus Maximus
  • Assisi & Padua: St. Francis & St. Anthony Basilicas
  • Venice Lagoon & Swiss Alps (Mt. Titlis Revolving Rotair)
  • Germany Black Forest & Cologne Cathedral
  • Amsterdam Canal Cruise & Keukenhof Tulip Gardens
  • Brussels Atomium & Paris City Tour (Eiffel Tower & Sacré-Cœur)
  • Lourdes: Massabielle Grotto, Holy Baths & Candlelight Procession
  • Avila Fortress & Sanctuary of Our Lady of Fatima
  • Lisbon: Jerónimos Monastery & Belém Tower

Trip Specifications

  • Duration: 16 Days / 15 Nights (13 Cities)
  • Countries: Italy, Vatican City, Switzerland, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, France, Spain, Portugal
  • Accommodation: 3★ & 4★ Hotel Stays with Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
  • Transport: Sightseeing & Long-Distance in Luxury AC Coaches
  • Tour Manager: Professional, friendly, caring and efficient Malayali / English Tour Manager throughout
  • Extras: Travel Insurance (up to 70 yrs), Daily 500ml Mineral Water, GST & Visa Assistance

✈️ Arrival: Assemble at Cochin International Airport (or departure airport) for Emirates flight to Dubai, connecting to Rome. Arrive at Rome International Airport. Upon arrival after immigration formalities, meet our assistant and transfer to the Hotel in Rome.

🏛️ City Tour: Transfer for an exhilarating city tour with an English-speaking local guide, showcasing best-loved sights like Colosseum (photo stop), Circus Maximus, Tiber River, Roman Forum & key Roman monuments.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Rome Area.

🇻🇦 Papal Audience: Attend the blessed morning Papal Audience in Vatican City (subject to Holy Father's schedule). Experience the spiritual unity of believers from around the world.

⛪ Vatican Splendors: Visit the breathtaking St. Peter's Square and the iconic St. Peter's Basilica, the largest church in Christendom, housing Michelangelo's Pietà.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Rome.

🕊️ Assisi Pilgrimage: Travel through the serene Umbrian countryside to the holy hilltop town of Assisi. Visit the world-famous Basilica of St. Francis of Assisi containing sacred Giotto frescoes and the tomb of the saint.

🚌 Journey to Padua: Continue north toward the historic city of Padua.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Padua / Veneto Area.

⛪ Padua St. Anthony: Morning visit to the grand Pontifical Basilica of Saint Anthony in Padua, venerating the beloved patron saint.

🚤 Venice Lagoon: Transfer to Tronchetto and board a private boat to St. Mark's Square. Admire St. Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, and the Bridge of Sighs.

🇨🇭 Cross into Switzerland: In the afternoon, embark on a scenic coach ride across the majestic Alps into Switzerland.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Zurich / Swiss Area.

🏔️ Mt. Titlis Rotair: Travel to Engelberg and ascend to 3,020 meters on the world's first revolving Titlis Rotair cable car. Enjoy the Glacier Cave, Titlis Cliff Walk suspension bridge, and panoramic alpine peaks.

🇨🇭 Alpine Lucerne: Afternoon visit to historic Lucerne to see the iconic Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke), the poignant Lion Monument, and Lake Lucerne.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Switzerland.

🌲 Black Forest & Lake Titisee: Cross the German border into the fairy-tale Black Forest. Visit Lake Titisee and see a demonstration of traditional German cuckoo clock crafting.

⛪ Cologne Cathedral: Proceed north along the Rhine Valley to Cologne. Visit the monumental twin-spired Cologne Cathedral (Kölner Dom), home to the Shrine of the Three Holy Kings.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Cologne Area.

🇳🇱 Amsterdam Canal Cruise: Cross into the Netherlands. Enjoy a scenic Amsterdam Canal Cruise gliding past historic gabled townhouses, skinny bridges, and lively waterways.

🌷 Tulip Gardens & Windmills: Discover traditional Dutch heritage, windmills, cheese making, or the vibrant Keukenhof tulip displays (in season).

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Eindhoven / Netherlands Area.

🇧🇪 Brussels Highlights: Drive to the Belgian capital. Photo stop at the futuristic Atomium, explore the majestic Grand Place, and see the famous Manneken Pis.

🇫🇷 Welcome to Paris: In the afternoon, travel across the border into France and check-in to your hotel in Paris.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Paris Area.

🗼 Paris Landmarks: Guided city tour of Paris including the Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Champs-Élysées, Place de la Concorde, and the banks of the Seine River.

⛪ Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur: Ascend the hill of Montmartre to visit the magnificent Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Paris (Sacré-Cœur) with panoramic city views.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Paris Area.

🚌 Journey to Pyrenees: Depart Paris on a scenic journey across southwest France towards the foothills of the Pyrenees mountains to the sacred Marian sanctuary town of Lourdes.

🕯️ Arrival in Lourdes: Check into your hotel, participate in an introductory visit to the Sanctuary and join the evening Rosary / Candlelight Procession.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Lourdes.

💧 Grotto of Massabielle: Full day dedicated to prayer and pilgrimage in Lourdes. Pray at the holy Grotto where Our Lady appeared to St. Bernadette Soubirous, drink from the miraculous healing spring, and participate in the Holy Baths gesture.

⛪ Sanctuary Basilicas & Procession: Visit the Rosary Basilica, Basilica of the Immaculate Conception, Underground Basilica of St. Pius X, the Way of the Cross, and attend the moving evening Candlelight Marian Procession.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Lourdes.

🇪🇸 Cross the Pyrenees into Spain: Travel southwest through northern Spain to the legendary medieval walled city of Avila.

🏰 St. Teresa of Avila: Discover the iconic Romanesque and Gothic walls with 88 towers. Visit the Convent and Church of Saint Teresa, built over the birthplace of the great mystic and Doctor of the Church.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Avila Area.

🇵🇹 Journey to Fatima: Cross the Portuguese border and drive towards the world-renowned Sanctuary of Our Lady of Fatima in Cova da Iria.

🕯️ Evening Candlelight Procession: Check-in at hotel, participate in the evening International Rosary at the Chapel of Apparitions, followed by the unforgettable Candlelight Procession across the massive sanctuary square.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Fatima.

⛪ Fatima Shrines: Morning Holy Mass and visit to the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary (containing tombs of St. Francisco, St. Jacinta, and Sister Lúcia), and the modern Basilica of the Most Holy Trinity.

🇵🇹 Lisbon Highlights: Drive to the Portuguese capital Lisbon. Visit Jerónimos Monastery, Belém Tower, Monument to the Discoveries, and the Church of Saint Anthony of Lisbon.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Lisbon Area.

🛍️ Leisure & Shopping: Morning at leisure for last-minute souvenir shopping in Lisbon.

✈️ Departure: Transfer to Lisbon International Airport. Complete check-in and immigration formalities, then board your Emirates flight to Dubai.

✈️ Overnight in flight.

✈️ Connection & Arrival: Transit in Dubai and board connecting Emirates flight to Cochin International Airport (or your home destination). Arrive home with holy blessings, cherished spiritual memories, and unforgettable European experiences.
