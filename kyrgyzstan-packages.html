Kyrgyzstan Package (4 Nights / 5 Days)

4 Nights / 5 Days • Code: KG2605

📍 Bishkek, White House, Oak Park, Ala Archa National Park, Alamedin Gorge, Chunqurcha Complex

Book 5 Days Package

🛬 Arrival & Airport Pick-Up: Airport pick-up from Bishkek International Airport and transfer to your hotel for check-in.

🏨 Overnight: Bishkek

🏛️ Bishkek City Tour: White House, Panfilov Entertainment Park, Oak Park, Historical Museum, Victory Square.

🏔️ Ala Archa Tour: Excursion to Ala Archa National Park. Optional shooting range at Baytik Village & Paragliding (weather permitting).

🏨 Overnight: Bishkek

🏞️ Alamedin Tour: Alamedin Gorge & Alamedin River. Lunch at 12 Kaminov restaurant at riverside.

🐴 Activities: Horse riding at Alamedin riverside and optional Hot Air Balloon flight experience.

🏨 Overnight: Bishkek

🏔️ Chunqurcha Complex: Discover traditional complex Chunqurcha and enjoy lunch at Supara Restaurant.

🚠 Adventure Activities: Chunkurchak cable car ride, zipline, and optional paragliding (weather permitting).

🏨 Overnight: Bishkek

🛫 Farewell Kyrgyzstan: Check out from hotel and private transfer to Bishkek International Airport for your departure flight.
