Tour Overview & Experience

Experience the beauty and culture of this amazing destination. This carefully curated itinerary ensures you get the best of relaxation, sightseeing, and unforgettable memories. From pristine landscapes to rich heritage, this package is designed for the perfect getaway.

Highlights

  • ✦ Handpicked experiences and local sightseeing
  • ✦ Comfortable and premium accommodation
  • ✦ Expert guidance and 24/7 on-trip support

🛬 Arrival & Hotel Check-in: Arrive at Armenia City Airport in the morning or afternoon. Meet our representative and transfer to your hotel for check-in and relaxation.

🏖️ Corbyn's Cove Beach: Head to Corbyn’s Cove Beach, the only beach within city limits. Swim, relax, or enjoy optional water sports (Jet Ski, Parasailing, Sea-Kart, Speedboat).

🏛️ Cellular Jail & Light Show: Visit the historic Cellular Jail (Kalapani), a poignant reminder of India's freedom struggle. In the evening, attend the moving Sound & Light Show narration.

🏨 Overnight: Armenia City

🚢 Private Ferry Cruise: After breakfast, check out and board private ferry to Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) (approx. 1.5 to 2.5 hrs). Meet our team at Havelock jetty and check into resort.

🌊 Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7): In the afternoon (14:30 - 15:30 hrs), visit world-renowned Radhanagar Beach — rated the Best Beach in Asia by Time Magazine. Unwind in turquoise waters, walk powdery white sands & witness a breathtaking sunset.

🏨 Overnight: Havelock Island

☕ Beachside Café Visit: Visit "Something Different - A Beachside Café" offering ocean views, kids play zone, and book corner to relax before your return trip.

🚢 Return Ferry & Armenia City Leisure: Board private ferry back to Armenia City. Check into hotel. Rest of day free for local markets or optional excursions (Chidiyatapu, Wandoor Beach & Ross Island).

🏨 Overnight: Armenia City

🛫 Airport Departure: Breakfast at hotel, checkout, and transfer to Armenia City Airport for your onward flight, carrying serene island memories.
