Bali Bliss Escape

4 Nights / 5 Days • Code: ID2605-A

📍 Kuta, GWK Cultural Park, Uluwatu Temple, Kecak Dance, Bedugul, Tanah Lot Temple, Kintamani Volcano, Ubud, Bali Swings

Book Itinerary 1

🛬 Arrival & Transfer: Upon arrival at Ngurah Rai International Airport, private transfer to your hotel at Kuta. After check-in, rest of the day free for leisure.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🗿 GWK Cultural Park: Visit GWK Cultural Park by buggy car to view the giant Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue.

🌅 Uluwatu & Kecak Dance: Afternoon visit to cliffside Uluwatu Temple overlooking the Indian Ocean, followed by a sunset Kecak Dance performance.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🏔️ Bedugul Journey: Scenic drive to Bedugul highlands.

🌊 Tanah Lot Temple: Afternoon visit to iconic Tanah Lot sea temple perched on a rocky ocean outcrop for sunset views. Private vehicle available until 9:00 PM.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🌋 Kintamani Volcano: View Mount Batur & serene Batur Lake. Visit Ubud Village for traditional crafts and markets.

🌴 Bali Swings & Nest: Thrilling Bali Swings & Nest @ Pioneer Swings experience with jungle views.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🛫 Airport Transfer: Breakfast at hotel, checkout, and private transfer to airport for your departure flight.

Bali Honeymoon Escape

4 Nights / 5 Days • Code: ID2605-B

📍 Benoa Water Sports, Uluwatu Temple, Gitgit Waterfall, Handara Gate, Ayung River Rafting, Heaven Nest Swings

Book Itinerary 2

🌺 Welcome & Transfer: Warm Balinese welcome with garland and cold towel at Ngurah Rai Airport. Transfer to Kuta hotel. Evening at leisure on Kuta Beach for sunset views.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🏄 Benoa Water Sports: Full-day adventure at Benoa Beach with 1 round each of Banana Boat, Jet Ski, and Parasailing.

🛕 Uluwatu Temple: Afternoon visit to cliffside Uluwatu Temple and Kecak Fire Dance.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

💧 Gitgit Waterfall & Ulun Danu: Visit Gitgit Waterfall cascades. Continue to Ulun Danu Beratan Temple on Bedugul Lake.

⛩️ Handara Gate: Famous photo stop at iconic Handara Gate framed by misty hills.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🚣 Ayung River Rafting: White water rafting through tropical rainforests with traditional Indonesian lunch.

🌴 Swings & Heaven Nest: Romantic photos at Bali Swings & Heaven Nest Park in lush jungle scenery.

🏨 Overnight: Kuta, Bali

🛫 Departure Transfer: Leisure morning for last-minute shopping / beach. Transfer to Ngurah Rai Airport for departure flight.
