Kerala 5 Days Tour

📍 Cochin • Munnar • Alleppey

Experience this amazing itinerary.

🌍 Countries Covered:
India

Tour Overview & Experience

Experience the enchanting beauty of God's Own Country. This 5-day tour covers the colonial charm of Cochin, the rolling tea gardens of Munnar, and the tranquil backwaters of Alleppey in a traditional houseboat.

✨ Key Highlights

  • Handpicked experiences and sightseeing
  • Comfortable and premium accommodation
  • Expert guidance and 24/7 support

📋 Trip Specifications

  • Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights
  • Accommodation: 3/4 Star Hotel or Resort
  • Transfers: Private Car Transfers

Pick Up From Cochin Airport, Drive To Munnar. On The Way, You Can Visit The Elephant Training Centre In Kodanad And Watch The Elephants Bathing. Please Note That It Will Be Arrange If Elephants Are Shelter At Kodanad And You Can Enough Time After Your Arrival In Kochi Airport. You Can Also Watch The Waterfalls On The Way To Munnar, Like Valara Waterfalls And Cheeyappara Waterfalls. Check Into The Hotel In Munnar..

After Breakfast, Start A Sightseeing Tour Of Munnar. Munnar Drives Its Name From The Confluence Of Three Mountain Streams - Mudrapuzha, Nallathanni, And Kundale. Mattupetty Is A Popular Picnic Spot Where You Can Opt For Activities Like Boating And Horse Riding. Indo Swiss Dairy Farm Is A Little Ahead Of Mattupetty Lake Is Famous For Around 100 Varieties Of Cattle. Close By Is The Echo Point, Which Offers You A Panoramic View And The Thrill Of Hearing Your Voice Echo. Popular Sightseeing Spots In Munnar That Can Be Visited Also Include Poopara, Pothanmedu, Devikulam, Mattupetty Indo Swiss Project, Eravikulam National Park, Top Station, And Marayoor. Overnight Stay At The Munnar Hotel.

After Breakfast In The Hotel, Drive To Thekkady, Get Ready For A Full Day Sightseeing Of Thekkady. The Main Attraction In Thekkady Is Boating In The Lake Periyar Near The Mullaperiyar Dam. Boating Takes Around Two Hours, And If You Are Lucky And One Is Often Lucky, You Will Spot Wild Animals Herding In To Drink The Lake Water Particularly In The Early Hours Of The Day. Thekkady Is Also Known For Adventure Tourism. The Forest Department Offers Many Programs Including Periyar Tiger Trail, Nature Walk, Bamboo Rafting Etc. Overnight Stay In Thekkady Hotel.

Have Breakfast In Hotel, Drive To Alleppey, Check Into The Houseboat At 10:00 A.M., Houseboat Day Trip Can Start Any Time After 10:00 Hrs, And Make A Trip Through The Punnamada River. This River Is Famous For Nehru Trophy Boat Race Which Is Being Conducted Every Year. The Boat Enter Sndp Thod (Thod Means Canal), The Smallest Canal In This Trip (Width 30-35 Mtrs). During This 1 Hr Cruise, Boat Can Stop Some Of The Places, You Can Walk In The Village To See And Experience The Village Life. At The End Of This Canal, The Boat Will Stop For Lunch. The Trip Starts After Lunch And Proceed To Munnattumugam – Where Three Canals Joints Together Making Very Attractive And Beautiful Place. The Boat Cruise Through Kainakari, A Beautiful Village With Green Paddy Fields. One Can See Chavera Church – Those Interested Can Visit This Church. Boat Makes A Cruise Through Irrmbanam Thod (This Is Also A Small Canal). One Can Also See Both Side Paddy Fields. Boat Make A Trip Through C Block Canal And Reach Punnamada Jetty After 5 Hrs. Enjoy Dinner In The Houseboat. Overnight Stay In Houseboat.

Have Breakfast In Houseboat, Drive To Cochin, Transfer To Cochin Airport / Railway Station For Onward Journey.
