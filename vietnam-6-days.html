Tour Overview & Experience

Discover the best of Vietnam in 6 days! This carefully designed itinerary combines cultural heritage, surreal natural landscapes, and modern coastal delights. From private airport transfers upon arrival in Hanoi to exploring Hanoi Train Street, cruising through Halong Bay's limestone islands, riding basket boats in Hoi An, and enjoying the world-famous Ba Na Hills cable car.

✨ Key Highlights

  • Arrival transfer & evening walk at historic Hanoi Train Street
  • Full day Ninh Binh tour: Hoa Lu Ancient Capital, Tam Coc boat trip & Mua Cave climb
  • Halong Bay day cruise with buffet lunch onboard
  • Flight to Da Nang & Cam Thanh Coconut Village basket boat experience
  • Lantern-lit Hoi An ancient town tour with dinner included
  • Ba Na Hills excursion & Golden Bridge cable car ride

📋 Essential Summary

  • Trip Code: VN2606
  • Duration: 5 Nights / 6 Days
  • Accommodation: 5 Nights Hotel (Hanoi & Da Nang)
  • Transfers: Private Airport Transfers + SIC Excursions

✈️ Arrival: Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport in the afternoon and enjoy a private transfer without guide to your hotel in Hanoi.

☕ Evening Sightseeing: In the evening, visit the famous Hanoi Train Street and explore the lively cafés and local surroundings before returning for an overnight stay in Hanoi.

🏨 Overnight: Hanoi

🏞️ Excursion: After breakfast, join an SIC tour to Ninh Binh and visit the historic Hoa Lu Ancient Capital.

🛶 Boat Ride & Climb: Enjoy lunch and continue with a peaceful boat ride through Tam Coc followed by a climb to Mua Cave for breathtaking panoramic views before returning to Hanoi for an overnight stay.

🏨 Overnight: Hanoi

⛵ Day Cruise: Enjoy breakfast and proceed on an SIC excursion to Halong Bay for a scenic day cruise through stunning limestone islands and emerald waters.

🍱 Onboard Lunch: Relish lunch onboard while exploring caves and beautiful viewpoints before returning to Hanoi in the evening for an overnight stay.

🏨 Overnight: Hanoi

✈️ Flight & Transfer: After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your flight to Da Nang.

🚣 Coconut Village & Hoi An: Upon arrival, continue on an SIC tour to Cam Thanh Coconut Village for a basket boat experience through the coconut forest, followed by a visit to the charming lantern-lit streets of Hoi An with dinner included before returning to Da Nang for an overnight stay.

🏨 Overnight: Da Nang

🚠 Cable Car & Golden Bridge: Following breakfast, visit Ba Na Hills and enjoy a spectacular cable car ride.

🌆 Sightseeing: After lunch and sightseeing, return to Da Nang for a relaxing overnight stay.

🏨 Overnight: Da Nang

🛫 Onward Flight: After breakfast, enjoy free time until your private transfer without guide to Da Nang International Airport for your onward departure from Vietnam.

✨ End of Tour & Safe Travels!

✓ What Is Included

  • Private airport transfers without guide (Hanoi Noi Bai Airport & Da Nang Airport)
  • SIC day tour to Ninh Binh (Hoa Lu, Tam Coc boat ride & Mua Cave)
  • SIC excursion to Halong Bay day cruise with lunch onboard
  • SIC tour to Cam Thanh Coconut Village basket boat & Hoi An lantern town with dinner
  • SIC excursion to Ba Na Hills with cable car ride & lunch included
  • Domestic flight ticket from Hanoi to Da Nang
  • Daily breakfast at hotels

✕ What Is Excluded

  • International airfare & Vietnam entry visa fees
  • Personal expenses, tipping & drinks during meals
  • Travel insurance
  • Early check-in or late check-out at hotels
  • Anything not explicitly mentioned under Inclusions
