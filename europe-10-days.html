Trip Specifications

  • Duration: 10 Days / 9 Nights

Welcome to Rome, the captivating capital of Italy! Upon arrival, we will dive straight into the historic center of the city.

  • Explore the awe-inspiring Colosseum, an iconic symbol of ancient Roman grandeur.
  • Wander through the Roman Forum, the bustling heart of ancient Rome.
  • Ascend Palatine Hill and marvel at the panoramic vistas of the city below.

Journey to Florence, renowned for its artistic treasures and Renaissance splendor.

  • Visit the world-famous Uffizi Gallery housing masterpieces by Michelangelo, Botticelli, and Raphael.
  • Discover the architectural marvel of Florence Cathedral (Duomo).
  • Stroll across the iconic Ponte Vecchio bridge and explore Oltrarno.

Experience romantic canals and timeless charm in the floating city of Venice.

  • Enjoy a traditional gondola ride through picturesque canals.
  • Visit St. Mark’s Square and St. Mark’s Basilica.
  • Explore the historic Doge’s Palace and Rialto Bridge.

Cross into Switzerland to explore Zurich, blending historical charm with alpine beauty.

  • Stroll through Zurich's historic Old Town (Altstadt).
  • Relax on Lake Zurich with scenic mountain views.
  • Indulge in authentic Swiss chocolates.

Ascend the snow-capped peak of Mount Titlis on the Rotair cable car.

  • Cross Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke) and see Lion Monument in Lucerne.
  • Walk through Titlis Cliff Walk - Europe's highest suspension bridge.

Visit Interlaken, nestled between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz.

  • Explore alpine villages and optional train ride to Jungfraujoch.
  • Free time for paragliding and shopping.

Visit Mozart's birthplace and iconic Mirabell Gardens in Salzburg.

  • Explore Salzburg Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage Site).
  • View Hohensalzburg Fortress perched high above the city.

Experience imperial grandeur in Vienna.

  • Tour Schönbrunn Palace and St. Stephen's Cathedral.
  • Visit Belvedere Palace and Hofburg Palace.

Discover Bavarian culture in Munich.

  • Visit Marienplatz and witness the famous Glockenspiel performance.
  • Tour Nymphenburg Palace and Viktualienmarkt.

Conclude your journey in Amsterdam's iconic canal ring.

  • Enjoy a relaxing glass-topped canal boat cruise.
  • Visit Dam Square and Van Gogh Museum before transfer to airport.
