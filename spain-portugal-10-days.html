Spain & Portugal Discovery

10 Days / 9 Nights 2 Countries & Iberian Highlights ✨ UNESCO Heritage Tour

📍 Seville (2N) → Algarve/Lagoa → Lisbon (2N) → Sintra → Fatima → Porto (1N) → Salamanca → Madrid (2N) → Toledo → Barcelona (2N)

Enjoy the beautiful, sun-drenched cities of Spain and Portugal in a single captivating tour.

🌍 Countries Covered:
Spain Portugal

Tour Overview & Experience

Experience the magic of the Iberian Peninsula on this signature 10-day Spain and Portugal discovery journey. From the sun-drenched squares and flamenco passion of Seville to the golden coastal cliffs of the Algarve, the royal palaces of Sintra, the sacred Marian pilgrimage in Fátima, the picturesque riverside of Porto, the golden sandstone of Salamanca, the imperial capital of Madrid, the historic ramparts of Toledo, and Antoni Gaudí's modernist masterpieces in Barcelona.

Key Tour Highlights

  • Toledo: UNESCO World Heritage medieval walled city with synagogues, mosques & churches
  • Sagrada Família: Gaudí's UNESCO World Heritage towering modernist basilica in Barcelona
  • Casa Milà (La Pedrera): Iconic Gaudí architectural masterpiece on Passeig de Gràcia
  • Shrine of Fátima: Sacred Catholic Marian pilgrimage sanctuary & Chapel of Apparitions
  • Sintra: Fairy-tale town near Lisbon with Pena Palace & romantic castle gardens
  • Algarve / Lagoa: Golden beaches, dramatic sea cliffs & coastal caves of southern Portugal
  • Salamanca: Grand Plaza Mayor & historic Renaissance golden sandstone university
  • Seville & Madrid: Royal Alcázar, Seville Cathedral, Madrid Royal Palace & Puerta del Sol

Trip Specifications

  • Duration: 10 Days / 9 Nights (2 Countries)
  • Night Breakdown: Seville 2N, Lisbon 2N, Porto 1N, Madrid 2N, Barcelona 2N
  • Accommodation: 3★ & 4★ Hotel Stays with Daily Breakfast & Meals
  • Transport: Luxury AC Coach throughout Spain & Portugal
  • Tour Manager: Professional Tour Manager accompanying throughout
  • Validity: Scheduled group departures from Apr 2026 to Mar 2027

🇪🇸 Welcome to Seville: Arrive at Seville Airport / meeting point. Seville is the vibrant southern Spanish capital famous for fiery flamenco, authentic tapas, orange-tree-lined avenues, and centuries of Moorish heritage.

🏨 Check-in & Welcome Dinner: Meet your tour assistant, transfer to your hotel, and enjoy an authentic welcome dinner.

🏨 Overnight stay in Seville (Night 1 of 2).

🏛️ Seville Sights: Guided city tour of Seville including the monumental Seville Cathedral and Giralda Tower, the colorful tilework of Plaza de España, and Maria Luisa Park.

💃 Santa Cruz Quarter: Walk through the charming Jewish quarter of Santa Cruz with its narrow whitewashed alleys and flower-filled patios.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Seville (Night 2 of 2).

🌊 Algarve Coastal Wonders: Cross the border into southern Portugal's picturesque Algarve region. Marvel at the dramatic limestone cliffs, golden beaches, and turquoise waters around Lagoa.

🇵🇹 Journey to Lisbon: Continue north through the Alentejo plains to Portugal's coastal capital, Lisbon. Hotel check-in and evening dinner.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Lisbon Area (Night 1 of 2).

🏰 Fairy-Tale Sintra: Excursion to the UNESCO World Heritage town of Sintra, nestled in the pine-covered hills. Admire the eccentric romantic architecture of the colorful royal palaces and verdant gardens.

🇵🇹 Lisbon Highlights: Return to Lisbon for a guided tour of the Belém district: the iconic Belém Tower, Monument to the Discoveries, and the Manueline masterpiece Jerónimos Monastery.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Lisbon Area (Night 2 of 2).

🕊️ Shrine of Fátima: Travel to Fátima, one of the most important Catholic pilgrimage centers in the world. Visit the vast Sanctuary square, the Chapel of Apparitions, and the Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary.

🍷 Historic Porto: Continue north to Porto along the Douro River. Discover the UNESCO-listed Ribeira district, colorful riverside houses, and world-famous Port wine heritage.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Porto Area (Night 1 of 1).

🇪🇸 Golden Salamanca: Cross the border into Spain and stop in the historic university town of Salamanca. Admire its glowing golden sandstone Renaissance architecture and the magnificent Plaza Mayor, one of Spain's grandest squares.

👑 Capital Madrid: Continue across the Castilian plains to Madrid, Spain's imperial capital city. Hotel check-in and dinner.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Madrid Area (Night 1 of 2).

🏰 Toledo UNESCO Heritage: Excursion to Toledo, the ancient "City of Three Cultures". Explore its breathtaking medieval fortifications, Gothic cathedral, historic synagogues, and sword-crafting heritage.

👑 Madrid City Tour: Return to Madrid for a guided city tour: the Royal Palace (Palacio Real), Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Cibeles Fountain, and Gran Vía.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Madrid Area (Night 2 of 2).

🚌 Scenic Drive to Catalonia: Travel east through the Spanish countryside towards the sparkling Mediterranean coast and the cosmopolitan city of Barcelona.

🌊 Mediterranean Welcome: Hotel check-in followed by an evening orientation walk around Plaça de Catalunya and the lively tree-lined promenade of Las Ramblas.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Barcelona Area (Night 1 of 2).

⛪ Sagrada Família & Casa Milà: Guided architectural tour of Barcelona highlighting Antoni Gaudí’s UNESCO masterpieces: the extraordinary Sagrada Família basilica and the sculpted wavy stone façade of Casa Milà (La Pedrera).

🏛️ Gothic Quarter & Montjuïc: Stroll through the historic Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic) and enjoy panoramic city and harbor vistas from Montjuïc hill.

🏨 Farewell dinner and overnight stay in Barcelona Area (Night 2 of 2).

🛍️ Souvenirs & Airport Transfer: Morning free time for final shopping in Barcelona. Transfer to Barcelona El Prat Airport for your scheduled return flight home.

✈️ Arrival Home: Arrive home with magnificent memories of Spain and Portugal.

🏁 Tour concludes with wonderful Iberian memories.
