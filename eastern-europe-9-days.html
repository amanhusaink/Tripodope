Tour Overview & Experience

Discover the best-kept secrets of Eastern Europe and the Adriatic Coast on this spectacular 9-day journey spanning Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro, and Bosnia & Herzegovina. Visit Lake Bled with its cliffside castle and Pletna boat, explore the subterranean wonder world of Postojna Cave, listen to the sea waves playing Zadar's 'Sea Organ', walk the majestic fortified walls of Dubrovnik, sail across Montenegro’s Bay of Kotor to Our Lady of the Rocks, cross the historic Ottoman bridge in Mostar, experience the Roman Emperor Diocletian’s Palace in Split, and marvel at the 16 cascading turquoise terraced lakes of Plitvice National Park.

Key Tour Highlights

  • Plitvice Lakes National Park: UNESCO World Heritage 16 cascading turquoise lakes & waterfalls
  • Bled Castle & Lake Bled: Slovenia's oldest medieval cliff castle & traditional Pletna boat ride
  • Postojna Cave: 24km underground karst cave system with stalactite train ride
  • Dubrovnik (Pearl of the Adriatic): Historic UNESCO-walled city, marble Stradun & ramparts
  • Bay of Kotor & Perast (Montenegro): Fjord-like bay & boat ride to Our Lady of the Rocks islet
  • Mostar (Bosnia & Herzegovina): UNESCO 16th-century Ottoman Stari Most Old Bridge & oriental bazaar
  • Zadar: Wave-powered 'Sea Organ' and Sun Salutation on the Adriatic Sea
  • Split & Zagreb: Roman Emperor Diocletian's Palace & Croatia's historic capital Zagreb

🇸🇮 Welcome to Ljubljana: Arrive at Ljubljana Airport / meeting point. Ljubljana, the charming capital city of Slovenia, is renowned for its vibrant green spaces, dragon bridges, Baroque architecture, and a seamless blend of German, Mediterranean, and Slavic cultures.

🏨 Check-in & Dinner: Meet your tour manager, transfer to the hotel for check-in, and enjoy an authentic European welcome dinner.

🏨 Overnight stay in Ljubljana Area (Night 1 of 2).

🏰 Lake Bled & Bled Castle: Travel to the alpine wonderland of Lake Bled. Visit cliffside Bled Castle, Slovenia's oldest medieval landmark, offering breathtaking views over the lake and Julian Alps.

🛶 Pletna Boat Ride: Board a traditional hand-rowed wooden Pletna boat to reach the islet in the center of Lake Bled. Return to Ljubljana for an orientation tour of the Triple Bridge and Dragon Bridge.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Ljubljana Area (Night 2 of 2).

🕳️ Postojna Cave System: Explore one of the world's greatest karst wonders. Ride the subterranean electric train through 24 kilometers of magnificent stalactite and stalagmite halls.

🇭🇷 Coastal Zadar & Sea Organ: Cross the border into Croatia and arrive in ancient Zadar on the Adriatic Sea. Experience the unique 'Sea Organ', an architectural marvel where sea waves play natural musical chords through underwater pipes, and witness colorful sunsets.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Zadar Area (Night 1 of 1).

🌊 Scenic Dalmatian Coast: Drive southward along the crystal-clear Adriatic coastline past islands and turquoise coves to Dubrovnik.

🏰 Historic Dubrovnik Tour: Guided walking tour inside Dubrovnik's UNESCO World Heritage stone walls. Stroll along the limestone Stradun, view the Rector’s Palace, Onofrio's Fountain, Franciscan Monastery, and admire the iconic red-tiled roofs overlooking the sea.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Dubrovnik Area (Night 1 of 2).

🇲🇪 Fjord-like Bay of Kotor: Cross the border into Montenegro and drive around Europe's southernmost fjord, the breathtaking UNESCO Bay of Kotor.

⛵ Perast & Our Lady of the Rocks: Visit the historic village of Perast and take a boat ride to the picturesque artificial islet sanctuary of Our Lady of the Rocks. Explore Kotor’s medieval walled town and St. Tryphon Cathedral before returning to Dubrovnik.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Dubrovnik Area (Night 2 of 2).

🇧🇦 Fairy-Tale Mostar: Journey into Bosnia and Herzegovina to Mostar. Marvel at the world-famous UNESCO 16th-century Ottoman bridge (Stari Most) arched gracefully over the emerald Neretva River. Stroll through the cobblestone alleys of the old Turkish bazaar (Kujundžiluk).

🇭🇷 Coastal Split: Return to Croatia and travel north to the historic port city of Split for hotel check-in and dinner.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Split Area (Night 1 of 1).

🏛️ Roman Split: Morning walking tour inside the monumental Roman Emperor Diocletian’s Palace (UNESCO World Heritage Site), the Peristyle courtyard, and Jupiter’s Temple.

🌊 Plitvice Lakes National Park: Drive inland to Croatia’s crown jewel—Plitvice Lakes National Park. Wander along wooden boardwalks over 16 terraced emerald-and-turquoise lakes connected by dramatic roaring waterfalls.

🏨 Dinner and overnight stay in Plitvice Area (Night 1 of 1).

🇭🇷 Zagreb City Tour: Drive to Zagreb, Croatia's historic and vibrant capital. Explore the Upper Town (Gornji Grad), the iconic multi-colored tiled roof of St. Mark’s Church, Zagreb Cathedral, Lotrščak Tower, and the bustling Ban Jelačić main square.

🍷 Farewell Dinner: Celebrate a memorable Balkan tour with a traditional farewell dinner in Zagreb.

🏨 Overnight stay in Zagreb Area (Night 1 of 1).

🛍️ Final Shopping & Transfer: Morning at leisure in Zagreb for souvenirs and Croatian specialties. Transfer to Zagreb Franjo Tuđman Airport for your return flight home.

✈️ Arrival Home: Arrive home with magnificent memories of Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro, and Bosnia & Herzegovina.

🏁 Tour concludes with unforgettable Eastern European memories.
